## 1. What changed in the shared brief

`briefs/shared.md` is round 1's shared brief verbatim, plus a "Round 2" paragraph that (a) says the biome is the thing you browse and the tier is a property of the pin, (b) demands the exact 1536×708 frame (round 1's screens were ~2.0:1), (c) demands that Lab, Playgrounds and a locked track with its rule are all *inside the frame* (round 1's A lost the first two and D/E lost the third), and (d) moves the header / bottom-band text into the shared hard rules so the per-direction briefs only describe the map. The per-direction briefs each name the architecture, the pin design, where Lab and Playgrounds live, how the tier gates are drawn, the framing (B1, M1 and H1 all on screen), and the output path.

Tap model in every brief: a tap on a pin focuses it; the amber `RIDE` pill — or a second tap on the focused pin — launches. Counted from the menu (`PLAY` = tap 1), a track whose pin is already focused starts in **2 taps**; any other on-screen pin in **3** (or 2 with the double-tap rule); a locked pin's rule is on the pin itself, so reaching H1's rule is 2 taps when H1 is on screen.

### A1 — "One continuous map" (`A1-continuous-map.jpg`)

**What it is.** A single painted landscape, high three-quarter view, with the five biomes as regions that blend into each other left to right — Industrial docklands, Canyon, Snow, Night City, Foundry — and the region name with its count painted on the terrain (`INDUSTRIAL · 3 / 4` … `FOUNDRY 0 / 4`). Tracks are round map pins with the code and the medal disc; a road links them in tier order and is lit amber where it is cleared (B1 → B2 → B3 → E1 → E2 → E3), amber-dashed to M1 (`UP NEXT`), grey beyond. Locked territory is hatched; a `HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK` gate sign stands at the Night City border, an `EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK` sign at the right edge, and every locked pin carries its rule on a plate (H1 brighter, `NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`, `Target 1:25.000`). The Lab is a hangar in the bottom-left corner with a bike and ramps (`LAB · PHYSICS TEST`, `PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS`); the Playgrounds are five green flags, one per region (`P1 CONTAINER YARD · OPEN` …). A legend strip above the band: outlined `LAB · PHYSICS TEST` and `PLAYGROUNDS` chips, then `INDUSTRIAL 3/4 · CANYON 3/3 · SNOW 0/2 · NIGHT CITY 🔒 0/2 · FOUNDRY 0/4` with the amber underline on the region under the centre, and a `◀ PAN MAP ▶` hint. B1's popover: `FIRST RIDE · GOLD 0:41.200 · Target 1:05.000 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED`. Lower third as briefed, with a sponsor row beside the ticker.

**What it does with the brief.** The purest reading of "browse by biome": no panel borders, no captions, the terrain *is* the grouping and the road *is* the progression. Everything the shared brief demanded is in frame — Lab, all five playgrounds, B1, M1, H1 with its rule, and the two gate signs. The legend strip doubles as the tab bar and the progress readout, so the region counts appear twice (terrain + strip); one can go.

**Mockup vs. brief.** The generator drew the screen at **1.7:1** — the worst frame of the round — so the map area is ~40 % taller than it will be on a 932×430 phone; read the pins as tighter and the terrain as a band. M3, H3, X2, X3 are off the right edge (Foundry shows only its heading), i.e. the map *does* pan even though the mockup's legend suggests otherwise. Otherwise complete: 11 of 15 tracks, every P flag, both gate signs, the Lab.

**844×390.** The map band is ~230 px tall; the pins (44 px) plus a name line fit in one row along a road that must stay within ±60 px of a centre line, so the road flattens into a wavy line — this becomes round-1 A without the panel borders. The legend strip and its region counts are the first thing to fold into the band's left half.

### A2 — "Tabbed atlas" (`A2-tabbed-atlas.jpg`)

**What it is.** One biome per page. The Industrial page is on screen: a wide key-art vista (IRONWORKS, VORTEX OIL), the page title `INDUSTRIAL · 3 / 4 CLEARED` with a four-segment bar, a white route with B1 (gold) → B2 (silver) → B3 (bronze) → M1 (dashed, `UP NEXT`) and tier signs (`BEGINNER` ×3, `MEDIUM`) over the pins, and at the route's end a steel signpost `NEXT TIER GATE → 🔒 HARD · H1 ROOFTOP WIRE · NIGHT CITY · LOCKED · NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`. The Playgrounds page peeks at the left edge (five biome slivers; `P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT · OPEN`, `P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR · OPEN`), the Canyon page peeks at the right (red rock, the E1 pin). Under the map a full-width mini-map strip of seven tiles: `LAB` (grid paper, wrench, `PROVING GROUND`), `PLAYGROUNDS` (five stripes, `OPEN`), `INDUSTRIAL 3 / 4` (active, amber underline), `CANYON 3 / 3` (silver · bronze · silver dots), `SNOW 0 / 2` (dashed dot + padlock), `NIGHT CITY 0 / 2` (two padlocks, `MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`), `FOUNDRY 0 / 4` (one dashed dot, three padlocks); page counter `3 / 7`. B1's popover as briefed. Lower third as briefed; the medal totals are drawn as the four medal icons with `0 PLATINUM · 1 GOLD · 3 SILVER · 2 BRONZE`.

**What it does with the brief.** The most *legible* of the five at phone size: one biome's key art fills the screen, four pins have room for name, PB, target, ghost tag and a tier sign each, and the whole world is still one glance away in the mini-map strip (every medal, every padlock, and the Hard rule under Night City). Lab and Playgrounds are pages of a visibly different kind *and* different-looking tiles. The signpost is the one idea worth stealing whatever ships: the next tier gate is named, with its rule, on the page the player is already looking at.

**Mockup vs. brief.** Drawn at 1.98:1 (~9 % taller than the phone). The peeking pages are wider than a real snap-peek would be (~15 % of the width each; 6–8 % is realistic). M1's PB is drawn as `PB —` under the name, fine. Complete otherwise — the only mockup of the round where nothing asked for is missing.

**844×390.** Page title + route + mini-map strip + band = four bands in 390 px: the vista compresses to ~150 px, which still holds a 44 px pin row with a one-line label; the mini-map tiles drop their medal dots and keep the count and the padlock. Two of the seven tiles (Lab, Playgrounds) can shrink to icon width.

### A3 — "Isometric diorama" (`A3-isometric-diorama.jpg`)

**What it is.** Five chunky terrain tiles on a dark water table, each a model-railway slab of its biome — a container yard with cranes (IRONWORKS), a red mesa, a snow peak with a cable car, neon rooftops (KESTREL TYRES), a foundry with a lava fall — with the engraved count on the front face (`INDUSTRIAL 3 / 4`, `CANYON 3 / 3`, `SNOW 0 / 2`, `NIGHT CITY 0 / 2`, `FOUNDRY 0 / 4`) and small bridges joining them; the Night City bridge and the Foundry edge are fenced with hazard tape and `HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK` / `EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK` signs. Pins stand up on posts with a name plate, PB and target: B1 (gold, focused, amber route line to it, popover `FIRST RIDE · GOLD · 0:41.200 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED · PB 0:41.200 / TARGET 1:05.000`), B2, B3, M1 (`UP NEXT`, `PB —`), E1–E3 (`PRO` on E3), M2, X1 🔒, H1 🔒 (`NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`), H2 🔒, M3, H3 🔒. The proving-ground island sits apart at the left: a NORDVIK hangar with a bike and floodlights (`LAB · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS · physics proving ground`) and five coloured pads `P1 CONTAINER YARD · OPEN` … `P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR · OPEN`. Page dots and `◀ ▶` under the table. Lower third as briefed.

**What it does with the brief.** The most *characterful* — the tiles read as a collectable world and the island makes "not part of the campaign" physical. Locks read as fences and barriers rather than grey pins; the amber route on the Industrial tile shows where the road idea would go.

**Mockup vs. brief.** This is the **retry**: the first run returned a **1870×841** image (2.22:1, no letterbox) instead of 1536×1024 — codex's own last message: *"The generator returned 1870 × 841, so the requested 1536 × 1024 dimensions and exact letterboxing were not met"* — and dropped every PB and target; it is kept as `A3-isometric-diorama-run1-wide.png/.jpg` because its island (a plinth plate, five square pads) and its `SWIPE LEFT / RIGHT TO EXPLORE` hint are worth a look. The retry landed **exactly on the 1536×708 frame** (158 px letterbox top and bottom, 2.17:1) — the only mockup of either round to do so. Both runs miss X2 and X3 (Foundry has M3 and H3 only, as round-1 A had) and neither greys the Night City tile as briefed (its pins are). The retry has two typos: `CONTAINER YARE` (H2) and `MEDAL EVERY HARM TRACK` (X1).

**844×390.** The honest problem: isometric tiles spend height on their *front faces* and depth, and at 390 px the map band is ~230 px, so each tile is a ~110 px thumbnail with 44 px pins nearly as big as the tile. Either the tiles flatten to near-top-down plates (then it is A1 with borders) or one tile is shown large at a time (then it is A2 with a 3-D page). The island as a separate object survives either way.

### AD — "Map + medal shelf" (`AD-map-medal-shelf.jpg`)

**What it is.** Round-1 A's panel strip — a `LAB` pegboard with one taped graph-paper ticket (`LAB · PHYSICS TEST · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS`), a `PLAYGROUNDS` rack of five biome-tinted tickets stamped `OPEN`, then the `INDUSTRIAL`, `CANYON`, `SNOW`, `NIGHT CITY` key-art panels (Foundry off the right edge) — with D folded in at the bottom of every biome panel: a brushed-steel **medal shelf** with one slot per track. Industrial's shelf holds a gold, a silver and a bronze medal on ribbons plus one empty amber-dashed slot (M1); Canyon's holds silver · bronze · silver on a gold-edged shelf stamped `COMPLETE`; Snow's has a dashed slot and a padlocked slot; Night City's two padlocked slots. The focused track is a **trophy plaque** hung in the Industrial panel: `B1 · FIRST RIDE`, `GOLD · 0:41.200 · TARGET 1:05.000`, an engraved elevation profile with checkpoint dots and a `▲ 43 m` climb, `PB 0:41.200 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED`. Pins on the route keep the code; H1 carries `NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`, H2 and X1 `MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM/HARD TRACK`.

**What it does with the brief.** The shelf answers "how am I doing in this biome?" at a glance without a count — a full shelf glows, an empty slot is a to-do, a padlocked slot is a not-yet — and the plaque gives the focused track more room than any popover (profile, PB, target, chips) without covering the pins. Lab and Playgrounds are unmistakably a different kind of panel (pegboard, ticket rack) *and* have no shelf, which is what says "no medals".

**Mockup vs. brief.** The generator renamed tracks: E1 is drawn `RED ROCKS`, E2 `THE LEDGE`, M2 `FROZEN PASS` (should be Uphill Weight, Rear Wheel First, Drum Roll); the build stamp reads `63eb25`; pin labels dropped the PB (it is on the plaque only). Foundry, M3, H3, X2, X3 are off the right edge. Drawn at 2.03:1.

**844×390.** The shelf costs ~70 px of every panel's height, on top of the panel caption it replaces (net ~+40 px); at 390 px the panel is ~230 px, so the route row and the shelf row are two ~100 px bands — tight but they clear 44 px pins and 44 px medals. The plaque cannot sit inside the panel at that height: it becomes the popover of round-1 A, or the band's left third. Five or six panels visible.

### AE — "Map + progression road" (`AE-map-road.jpg`)

**What it is.** One painted landscape with the region names and counts painted on the sky (`INDUSTRIAL 3 / 4 · CANYON 3 / 3 · SNOW 0 / 2 · FOUNDRY 0 / 4 · NIGHT CITY 0 / 2` — Foundry before Night City, so the road can reach M3 without crossing a locked region) and **one road** through all of it, every track a start-gate stop with a plate: code, name, PB and target (`B1 FIRST RIDE 0:41.200 / 1:05.000` … `H2 CONTAINER YARD 1:25.000`). The cleared road is lit amber from B1 through E3; M1's gate flies `UP NEXT · NEXT STOP`; the rider's bike is at E3 under `YOU ARE HERE · E3 STAIRWAY · PRO`. Barriers: a `HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK` gate before H3 in Foundry, an `EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK` sign at the X1 spur, H1 lit with `NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`. The Lab shed with a chequered flag at the road's start (`LAB · PHYSICS TEST · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS`); five side roads to `P1` … `P5`, each flagged `OPEN`. An overview minimap strip above the band: the road as a line, region names, medal dots / hollow dots / padlocks, P drop-offs as ticks, the bike, and an amber bracket `VISIBLE AREA (PAN LEFT / RIGHT)`. Lower third as briefed.

**What it does with the brief.** The only mockup of the round with **all 22 tracks in frame** — every code, PB, target, lock and playground — and the only one where progression is a *distance* (how far the amber goes, where the bike is) rather than a count. The minimap makes the pan honest. The road order (Industrial → Canyon → Snow → Foundry → Night City) is a real constraint discovered while briefing: a geographic road cannot follow tier order exactly, so within a region stops are in tier order and M1 sits after B3 — the amber segment is therefore B1–B3 and E1–E3, not one contiguous run, which reads fine.

**Mockup vs. brief.** Drawn at **2.43:1** — wider than the phone — with 22 tracks across it, so the plates are ~9 px type at phone scale; on a real 932×430 screen the same density is unreadable and the map *must* pan (about 2.5 screens). The minimap's bracket already says so. Complete otherwise; no missing elements, no garbled text.

**844×390.** Sky captions + gate row + plate row + minimap + band = five bands in 390 px: the gate arches become decoration behind the plates, the plates become the 44 px targets (as round-1 E), the region names move onto the minimap, and the minimap itself must be ≤ 24 px or fold into the band. This is the hardest of the five to fit short phones, as E was in round 1.

## 4. What the generator could not render (and the one retry)

- **Frame aspect, again.** Despite the shared brief's explicit 1536×708 rule, only AE overshot (2.43:1); A2 and AD landed near round 1's ~2.0:1; **A1 drew 1.7:1** (a near-16:9 screen with a 40 % taller map than the phone has) and **A3 came back as a 1870×841 image** — codex's own report: *"The generator returned 1870 × 841, so the requested 1536 × 1024 dimensions and exact letterboxing were not met."* The `image_gen` tool evidently chooses its own canvas. The 844×390 rows in § 3 are reasoned from the layouts and today's measured stylesheet, not rendered.
- **A3 retry.** Per the round rule (retry once on a failed or off-spec run) A3 was rerun with the same briefs (211 s): the retry came back 1536×1024 with the exact 158 px letterbox and is the canonical `A3-isometric-diorama.png`; the first run is kept as `A3-isometric-diorama-run1-wide.png` (1870×841). Nothing else was retried; no run refused the image or errored (all six exits 0).
- **Off-frame state.** A1 and AD lose Foundry's tracks (M3, H3, X2, X3) past the right edge; A3 drew M3 and H3 but no X2 / X3; A2 shows only the Industrial page by design (Night City's rule is on its tile and on the signpost). AE is the only one with all 22 on screen — at a density the phone cannot show.
- **Text.** AD renamed three tracks (`RED ROCKS`, `THE LEDGE`, `FROZEN PASS`) and misspelled the build stamp (`63eb25`); AD's pins dropped the PB; A3 has `CONTAINER YARE` and `MEDAL EVERY HARM TRACK` (its first run dropped PB and target instead). No garbled ticker entries this round.
- **Naming collision** from round 1 still stands: `h2-gap-chain` and `p1-container-yard` are both **Container Yard** (`src/tracks/courses/hard.ts`, `playgrounds.ts`); A1, A3 and AE draw both names on the same screen.

## 5. Recommendation (for the user to accept or reject)

**Build A2 (tabbed atlas) as the skeleton, and put A1's roads-and-gates and AD's shelf on its pages.** Reasons, in order:

1. **It fits the phone.** A2 is the only one of the five whose 844×390 story needs no new idea — one biome per page, ≤ 4 pins, a 44 px strip. A1, A3, AD and AE all have a "the map band is 230 px" problem to solve *after* the mockup, and A3 and AE have not solved it in this round either.
2. **It is the cheapest and the closest to the code that exists.** Seven pages are today's seven `tier-row`s turned into snap pages; the mini-map strip is the existing `FocusList`; the opening focus is `nextTrack()`. ~1–1.5 days, mostly CSS and three keyart plates.
3. **The signpost solves the round-1 A weakness** (no "what's next" beyond the amber pin): the Industrial page tells the player the next tier gate, its track, its biome and its rule without a tap.
4. The things the user liked in D and E survive as *page furniture* rather than as architecture: a shelf at the foot of each page (AD's idea, one element) and A1/AE's amber road through the page's pins (one SVG polyline per page, no cross-page road to reconcile with tier order).

If the user prefers the "one world" feel over the "one biome at a time" feel, **A1 is the second pick** and AE the third; A1 is A1 because its 844×390 fallback (a flatter road in one band) is at least known, whereas AE has five stacked bands. AD's shelf is a feature, not an architecture — bolt it onto whichever ships. A3 is the best-looking and the worst-fitting; keep its island (Lab + Playgrounds as a separate object) as the *concept* for how the proving ground looks on any of the others.

## 6. Notes for whoever builds it

- **Keep one scroller.** Every direction here is `overflow-x` on one element and nothing else; the mini-map / legend / minimap strips are `FocusList`s bound to the *same* index or scroll position, never a second scroll container. AE's side roads and A1's spur roads are positioned children of the scroller, not a row of their own.
- **Pin = the tap target, everything else is decoration.** 44 px minimum, code + medal disc inside, name under; the popover / plaque never sits over the pin row (A1, A2, A3 draw it above; AD hangs it inside the panel; AE pins it above the gate).
- **Opening focus.** `nextTrack(ship, medalOf, dev, lastPlayed)` already picks last-played-or-next; open on its page / region and centre it, then B1-class tracks are reachable in 2 taps.
- **Locked pins state their rule** as a plate on the pin (`Medal every ${TIER_LABEL[prev]} track` — the string exists in `card()` as `lockLine`); the tier-level sign (gate, signpost, fence) is *additional*.
- **Art already on disk** (`public/art`): `menu/keyart-{industrial,canyon}-{960,1920}.webp` (two biomes only), `plates/plate-<biome>.webp` + `sky-<biome>.webp` and `menu/results-<biome>.webp` for all five, `menu/track-*.webp` per-track cards, `thumbs/*.webp` per-track thumbs, `menu/medal-*.png` discs. A1 / AE need five contiguous panels that blend; A2 needs three more keyart plates; A3 needs six isometric plates; AD can reuse the `plate-*` set.
- **Data the mockups assume that the code does not have yet:** a per-biome track list in display order (derive: `ship.filter(t => t.meta?.biome === b)` ordered by `TIER_ORDER` then index), a pin anchor table per biome (A1/A3/AD/AE), and for AE the road's region order `industrial, canyon, snow, foundry, nightCity` as a constant with a comment on why it is not the biome enum order.
- **Rename one Container Yard** before any of these ship (H2 or P1).
