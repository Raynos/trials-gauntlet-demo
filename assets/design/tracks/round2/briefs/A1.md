## DIRECTION A1 — "One continuous map" (biome map, variant 1)
Architecture: ONE continuous painted MAP — a single illustrated landscape seen from a high three-quarter angle, like a race-weekend course map or a national-park trail map — fills the middle ~70 % of the screen height (from under the header to the top of the bottom band) and is WIDER than the screen: it pans LEFT-RIGHT, the only scroll axis. There are no panel borders and no per-panel caption bars: the five biomes are REGIONS of one land that blend into each other along the pan — INDUSTRIAL docklands and container yards on the left, CANYON red rock next, SNOW peaks, NIGHT CITY rooftops with neon, FOUNDRY black iron and molten pools on the far right. Each region carries a large painted region name on the terrain in the display face with its count under it: "INDUSTRIAL · 3 / 4", "CANYON · 3 / 3", "SNOW · 0 / 2", "NIGHT CITY · 0 / 2", "FOUNDRY · 0 / 4".
Tracks are NUMBERED PINS standing on the map (44 px round pins with a tail, like map markers): the pin's head shows the track code (B1, E2, M1 …) and its MEDAL DISC — gold/silver/bronze for cleared, a dashed empty ring for open-but-uncleared, a small padlock for locked. A small label under each pin: name and PB in tiny caps. Place them: INDUSTRIAL B1 (gold) B2 (silver) B3 (bronze) M1 (dashed ring, amber "UP NEXT" flag); CANYON E1 (silver) E2 (bronze) E3 (silver, tiny blue PRO chip); SNOW M2 (dashed ring) X1 (padlock); NIGHT CITY H1 (padlock, lit brighter, small plate "NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK") H2 (padlock, "MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK"); FOUNDRY M3 (dashed ring) H3 (padlock) X2 X3 (padlocks, "MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK").
TIER GATES are drawn as ROADS and BRIDGES between regions: a road links the pins in tier order (B1 → B2 → B3 → E1 → E2 → E3 → M1 → M2 → M3 …); where the road crosses into locked territory it passes a closed barrier gate — one across the road into NIGHT CITY (a steel gate with the sign "HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK") and a second, greyer, on the spur roads to X1 / X2 / X3 ("EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK"). The road segments between cleared pins are lit amber; the segment to M1 is amber-dashed; locked regions are desaturated with a faint diagonal hatch over the terrain.
LAB and PLAYGROUNDS on the same map but clearly a different kind of thing: the LAB is a dark test hangar with a grid floor drawn in the map's bottom-left corner, off the road, with one pin "LAB · PHYSICS TEST" and a small plate "PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS"; the PLAYGROUNDS are five small triangular FLAG markers (not pins), one planted at the edge of each region, reading "P1 CONTAINER YARD · OPEN", "P2 CANYON RUN · OPEN", "P3 SNOW LINE", "P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT", "P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR".
A thin LEGEND STRIP sits directly above the bottom band: five region chips "INDUSTRIAL 3/4 · CANYON 3/3 · SNOW 0/2 · NIGHT CITY 🔒 0/2 · FOUNDRY 0/4" (tap = pan there; the region under the centre of the screen has the amber underline), plus "LAB" and "PLAYGROUNDS" as two outlined chips at the strip's left end.
Framing: the visible span runs from the Lab hangar and Industrial (left) through Canyon and Snow to the Night City gate (right edge), so B1, M1 and H1 are all on screen; Foundry continues off the right edge. B1's pin has an amber focus ring and a popover above it: "FIRST RIDE · GOLD 0:41.200 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED". Header, bottom band and ticker as in the shared brief.
