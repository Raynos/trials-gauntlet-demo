## DIRECTION A2 — "Tabbed atlas" (biome map, variant 2)
Architecture: the map is an ATLAS of seven full-screen PAGES, one biome per page, that swipe LEFT-RIGHT with snapping — the only scroll axis. Page order: LAB · PLAYGROUNDS · INDUSTRIAL · CANYON · SNOW · NIGHT CITY · FOUNDRY. The INDUSTRIAL page is the one on screen (centred, about 80 % of the screen width) with the PLAYGROUNDS page peeking in at the left edge and the CANYON page peeking in at the right edge, so the swipe is obvious.
The page: a wide painted key-art vista of the biome (industrial docklands: IRONWORKS and VORTEX OIL signage, containers, sodium lamps) filling the map area between the header and the mini-map strip. A page title at the page's top-left in the display face: "INDUSTRIAL" with "3 / 4 CLEARED" and a 4-segment progress bar beside it (3 amber segments, 1 grey). A ROUTE — a thin white road/line — runs across the page from left to right and the biome's tracks sit on it in TIER ORDER as 44 px round PINS with the code and the medal disc inside: B1 (gold) → B2 (silver) → B3 (bronze) → M1 (dashed empty ring, amber "UP NEXT" flag). Under each pin the name and PB in tiny caps. Tier boundaries along the route are small painted road markings with a tier sign: "BEGINNER" over the first three, "MEDIUM" over M1. At the page's right end the route runs off the page into a SIGNPOST pointing right: "NEXT TIER GATE → HARD · H1 ROOFTOP WIRE · NIGHT CITY · LOCKED · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK" with a padlock glyph — this is how the locked next unlock is visible from the current page.
MINI-MAP STRIP along the bottom of the map area, directly above the bottom band, full width: seven small tiles, each 44 px tall, one per page, in page order: "LAB" (a grid-paper tile with a wrench glyph, "PROVING GROUND"), "PLAYGROUNDS" (a tile split into five thin biome-colour stripes, "OPEN"), "INDUSTRIAL 3/4" (active: amber underline and brighter), "CANYON 3/3" (three medal dots silver bronze silver), "SNOW 0/2" (one dashed dot, one padlock), "NIGHT CITY 0/2" (two padlocks, tile greyed, tiny caption "MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK"), "FOUNDRY 0/4" (one dashed dot, three padlocks). Tapping a tile jumps to that page. Small page dots "3 / 7" near the strip.
The peeking PLAYGROUNDS page at the left edge shows it is a different kind of page: five thin vertical biome slivers with P-pins (only P4 and P5 need to be legible: "P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT", "P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR", each stamped "OPEN"). The peeking CANYON page at the right edge shows red sandstone and the E1 pin (silver).
Focus: B1's pin has an amber ring and a popover above it: "FIRST RIDE · GOLD 0:41.200 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED". Header, bottom band and ticker as in the shared brief.
