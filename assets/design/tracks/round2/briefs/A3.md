## DIRECTION A3 — "Isometric diorama" (biome map, variant 3)
Architecture: the world as a tabletop DIORAMA seen in isometric view — five chunky RAISED TILES of terrain (thick slabs with visible cut sides, like model-railway or board-game tiles) sitting on a dark charcoal table, laid in a row that recedes slightly to the right, plus a separate smaller PROVING-GROUND ISLAND. The diorama is wider than the screen and pans LEFT-RIGHT, the only scroll axis. The map area fills the middle ~70 % of the screen height.
The five biome tiles, left to right: INDUSTRIAL (a cut-away container yard with cranes, rust and sodium lamps, an IRONWORKS sign), CANYON (red sandstone mesa with a sunset wash), SNOW (white peaks, pines, a tiny cable car), NIGHT CITY (rooftops with cyan/magenta neon, KESTREL TYRES billboard), FOUNDRY (black iron with molten orange channels). Each tile has an engraved name plate on its front cut side: "INDUSTRIAL 3 / 4", "CANYON 3 / 3", "SNOW 0 / 2", "NIGHT CITY 0 / 2", "FOUNDRY 0 / 4". Tiles are joined in tier order by small BRIDGES: cleared-to-cleared bridges lit amber; the NIGHT CITY tile is entirely desaturated grey with a closed barrier gate on its bridge and a sign "HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK"; on the SNOW and FOUNDRY tiles the locked pins stand on a greyed, hatched half of the tile behind a small fence signed "EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK".
Tracks are 44 px round PINS standing up on the tiles on short posts (map-marker style): the code and the medal disc inside — gold/silver/bronze for cleared, a dashed empty ring for open-but-uncleared, a padlock for locked — and the name in tiny caps on a small plate under the pin. INDUSTRIAL: B1 (gold) B2 (silver) B3 (bronze) M1 (dashed ring, amber "UP NEXT" flag); CANYON: E1 (silver) E2 (bronze) E3 (silver, tiny blue PRO chip); SNOW: M2 (dashed ring) X1 (padlock); NIGHT CITY: H1 (padlock, lit brighter, plate "NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK") H2 (padlock); FOUNDRY: M3 (dashed ring) H3 X2 X3 (padlocks).
The PROVING-GROUND ISLAND sits apart at the left, in front of and lower than the row, clearly not part of the chain: a smaller round tile with a dark test hangar on it (grid floor, a NORDVIK banner) with one pin "LAB · PHYSICS TEST" and the plate "LAB · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS", and around the hangar five small square practice PADS, each in its biome's colour with a flag: "P1 CONTAINER YARD", "P2 CANYON RUN", "P3 SNOW LINE", "P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT", "P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR", all stamped "OPEN".
Framing: the Industrial tile is nearest and largest (focused), the island at its left, Canyon and Snow beside it, the Night City tile with H1 and its plate at the right, Foundry cut by the right edge — B1, M1 and H1 all on screen. B1's pin has an amber ring and a popover above it: "FIRST RIDE · GOLD 0:41.200 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED". Header, bottom band and ticker as in the shared brief.
