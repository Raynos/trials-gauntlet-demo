## DIRECTION AD — "Biome map + medal shelf" (A + D hybrid)
Architecture: the BIOME MAP from round 1 is the stage — one horizontal strip of tall painted key-art PANELS, one per biome, filling the middle ~70 % of the screen height and scrolling LEFT-RIGHT, the only scroll axis — with the PODIUM WALL's medal idea folded in: medals are physical objects on a SHELF, the focused track is a TROPHY PLAQUE, and a completed biome reads as a filled shelf.
Panel order left to right: [LAB — a workshop PEGBOARD panel with one graph-paper ticket "LAB · PHYSICS TEST · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS" hung on it, no shelf] [PLAYGROUNDS — a rack of five thin biome-tinted tickets P1 CONTAINER YARD · P2 CANYON RUN · P3 SNOW LINE · P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT · P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR, each stamped "OPEN", no shelf] [INDUSTRIAL] [CANYON] [SNOW] [NIGHT CITY] [FOUNDRY]. The five biome panels are painted vistas in their biome colours with fictional sponsor signage.
In each biome panel a thin white ROUTE LINE snakes across the vista and the biome's tracks sit on it as 44 px round PINS with the code — INDUSTRIAL B1 B2 B3 M1 (M1 with an amber "UP NEXT" flag); CANYON E1 E2 E3 (E3 tiny blue PRO chip); SNOW M2 X1; NIGHT CITY H1 (lit brighter, small plate "NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK") H2; FOUNDRY M3 H3 X2 X3 — locked pins grey with a padlock, the panel region beyond the last open pin hatched.
THE SHELF: along the bottom of every biome panel runs a dark steel MEDAL SHELF (a ledge with a brushed-metal front edge and an engraved plate "INDUSTRIAL · 3 / 4" etc.) with one MEDAL SLOT per track in that biome, in track order. A cleared track's slot holds a real MEDAL on a hanging ribbon standing in the slot (gold with the code B1 engraved, silver B2, bronze B3; silver E1, bronze E2, silver E3); an open uncleared track's slot is an empty dashed outline with its code (M1 amber, M2, M3); a locked track's slot is a grey padlocked outline (X1, H1, H2, H3, X2, X3). The CANYON shelf is FULL (3 of 3) and its whole edge glows warm gold with a small "COMPLETE" stamp; the Industrial shelf has three medals and one empty amber slot; Snow, Night City and Foundry shelves are mostly empty and grey.
THE TROPHY PLAQUE: the focused track B1 does not get a popover; instead a brass-and-charcoal engraved PLAQUE (like a trophy base plate) sits in the Industrial panel above the route: "B1 · FIRST RIDE" in the display face, "GOLD · 0:41.200 · TARGET 1:05.000", a small engraved elevation-profile silhouette of the track with three checkpoint dots, and the chips "▶ GHOST" and green "2 / 6 NOTED". B1's pin has an amber ring and its gold medal on the shelf is lifted slightly forward with the same amber ring.
Framing: the PLAYGROUNDS rack is visible at the left edge (the Lab pegboard just beyond it may be cut by the edge), INDUSTRIAL and CANYON are fully visible and centred, SNOW next, NIGHT CITY with H1 and its plate visible at the right edge, so B1, M1 and H1 are all on screen. Header, bottom band and ticker as in the shared brief.
