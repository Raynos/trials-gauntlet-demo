## DIRECTION AE — "Biome map + progression road" (A + E hybrid)
Architecture: the BIOME MAP is the stage — ONE continuous painted landscape of five biome REGIONS that blend into each other and pan LEFT-RIGHT, the only scroll axis, filling the middle ~70 % of the screen height — and the PROGRESSION ROAD from round 1 runs through it: one ROAD, drawn on the terrain, enters at the left, passes through every region and leaves at the right, and every track is a STOP on the road. Region order along the road: INDUSTRIAL (docklands, containers, IRONWORKS sign) → CANYON (red sandstone at sunset) → SNOW (peaks, pines, a frozen lift) → FOUNDRY (black iron, molten channels, BOLT ENERGY sign) → NIGHT CITY (neon rooftops, KESTREL TYRES billboard) at the far right end. Each region has its name painted large on the terrain with its count: "INDUSTRIAL 3 / 4", "CANYON 3 / 3", "SNOW 0 / 2", "FOUNDRY 0 / 4", "NIGHT CITY 0 / 2".
STOPS: each track is a small START GATE on the road (a 44 px gate arch with a light tower) with a plate under it showing the code, the name and the medal disc (gold/silver/bronze; dashed empty ring when open and uncleared; padlock when locked) and the PB in tabular numerals. Stops in road order: INDUSTRIAL B1 (gold) B2 (silver) B3 (bronze) then M1 (dashed ring, an amber "UP NEXT" flag on its gate, plate "NEXT STOP"); CANYON E1 (silver) E2 (bronze) E3 (silver, tiny blue PRO chip); SNOW M2 (dashed ring) and, up a closed spur road to the lift, X1 (padlock, sign "EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK"); FOUNDRY M3 (dashed ring) then a BARRIER across the road with a sign "HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK" and behind it H3, X2, X3 grey with padlocks; NIGHT CITY, entered through a second closed gate, H1 (padlock, lit brighter, sign "NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK") and H2 (padlock).
THE ROAD'S LIGHT: the road segments under and between CLEARED stops (B1 → B2 → B3 and E1 → E2 → E3) are lit AMBER like a runway; the segment leading to M1 is amber-dashed; the open unridden segments (to M2, M3) are plain grey asphalt with white markings; the segments beyond the barriers are dark and desaturated with a faint hatch over the locked terrain. The rider's BIKE (a small trials bike, rider in a NORDVIK jersey) is parked at the furthest cleared stop, E3, with a small plate "YOU ARE HERE · E3 STAIRWAY · PRO".
LAB and PLAYGROUNDS on the same map but a different kind of thing: the LAB is a workshop SHED at the very start of the road on the far left, with a "LAB" sign and one small gate "PHYSICS TEST · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS"; the PLAYGROUNDS are five short SIDE ROADS branching off the main road, one in each region, each ending in a small open gate with a flag: "P1 CONTAINER YARD · OPEN", "P2 CANYON RUN · OPEN", "P3 SNOW LINE · OPEN", "P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT · OPEN", "P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR · OPEN".
A thin OVERVIEW MINIMAP of the whole road sits directly above the bottom band (not in the header): a horizontal line with the five region names, small dots for the stops (cleared dots filled, open dots hollow, locked dots padlocked), the P drop-offs as ticks below, and a bike dot at E3; the visible span is marked by an amber bracket. Tapping the minimap pans there.
Framing: the visible span runs from the Lab shed (left) through Industrial and Canyon to Snow, with the Foundry barrier and, at the right edge, the Night City gate and H1's plate, so B1, M1 and H1 are all on screen. B1's gate has an amber focus ring and a popover above it: "FIRST RIDE · GOLD 0:41.200 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED" — placed so it does not touch the badge plate. Header, bottom band and ticker as in the shared brief.
