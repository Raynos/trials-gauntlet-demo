Architecture: the camera sits on ONE biome tile at a time — the INDUSTRIAL tile, drawn HUGE and richly detailed (a full isometric diorama slab: cranes, container stacks, a forklift, sodium lamps, IRONWORKS and VORTEX OIL signage, an amber route line across it) filling about 70 % of the screen width and the whole map-area height. A bridge leaves the tile's right edge toward the next tile with a small signpost "NEXT TIER GATE → HARD · H1 ROOFTOP WIRE · NIGHT CITY · LOCKED · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK".
The OTHER FOUR tiles are SMALL miniatures in a row along the bottom edge of the map area (directly above the bottom band), each 44 px tall or more, each a tiny version of its diorama slab with its count and its pin discs as dots: "CANYON 3 / 3" (silver · bronze · silver), "SNOW 0 / 2" (dashed dot, padlock), "NIGHT CITY 0 / 2" (two padlocks, greyed, caption "MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK", the H1 padlock lit a touch brighter), "FOUNDRY 0 / 4" (dashed dot, three padlocks). The INDUSTRIAL miniature is in the row too, marked as the current one with an amber underline. Tapping a miniature FLIES the camera to that tile (the fly is the only navigation — one axis, left/right through the row; a swipe on the big tile does the same).
The PROVING-GROUND ISLAND sits TOP-RIGHT of the map area, small, apart from the row: the NORDVIK hangar with "LAB · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS" and the five coloured pads P1 CONTAINER YARD · P2 CANYON RUN · P3 SNOW LINE · P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT · P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR, all stamped "OPEN"; a tap on it flies there too.
