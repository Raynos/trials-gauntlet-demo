## DIRECTION A3c — "Tower" (tiles stacked by tier, the camera pans UP as you progress)
Architecture: the diorama tiles are stacked into a VERTICAL TOWER, one tile per TIER, BEGINNER at the bottom and EXTREME at the top, and the camera pans UP-DOWN — the only scroll axis. The tiles are staggered left / right as they climb (a zig-zag: Beginner bottom-left, Easy above it to the right, Medium above that to the left, Hard to the right, Extreme top-left, cut by the top edge) so that four levels fit the screen height, each level joined to the next by a short STAIR-BRIDGE. A tier tile keeps the diorama construction (thick slab, cut sides, engraved front plate) but its TERRAIN is split into its tracks' BIOMES: BEGINNER is all industrial (B1 gold · B2 silver · B3 bronze, plate "BEGINNER · 3 / 3"); EASY is all canyon (E1 silver · E2 bronze · E3 silver with PRO, "EASY · 3 / 3"); MEDIUM is three strips — industrial (M1, dashed ring, amber "UP NEXT" flag), snow (M2, dashed ring), foundry (M3, dashed ring) — "MEDIUM · 0 / 3 · UP NEXT"; HARD is night city (H1, H2) + foundry (H3), the whole tile DESATURATED GREY with the stair-bridge up to it barred by a hazard-tape gate carrying a padlock and "LOCKED · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK", H1 lit a touch brighter with its plate "NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK", plate "HARD · 0 / 3"; EXTREME (snow X1, foundry X2, X3) greyer still, its bridge gate reading "LOCKED · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK", plate "EXTREME · 0 / 3", mostly above the top edge. Pins as in the attached diorama: 44 px round pin on a post with the code and medal disc, name plate, PB and target under it. The amber route runs up the tower through the cleared pins and the lit stair-bridges; the bridge from Easy to Medium is amber-dashed.
The PROVING-GROUND ISLAND is at GROUND LEVEL beside the tower's base, bottom-right, on the water: the NORDVIK hangar "LAB · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS" and the five coloured pads P1 CONTAINER YARD · P2 CANYON RUN · P3 SNOW LINE · P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT · P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR, all stamped "OPEN".
A slim vertical TIER RAIL on the right edge of the map area (inside the safe area) shows the five tier names as 44 px stops with their counts and padlocks ("EXTREME 🔒 · HARD 🔒 · MEDIUM 0/3 · EASY 3/3 · BEGINNER 3/3"), the current level marked amber; a tap pans the camera there.
Framing: the camera is on the lower levels so B1 (bottom-left, focused, amber ring, popover "FIRST RIDE · GOLD 0:41.200 · TARGET 1:05.000 · ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED"), M1 (UP NEXT) and the grey HARD tile with H1's plate are all visible. Header, bottom band and ticker as in the shared brief.
