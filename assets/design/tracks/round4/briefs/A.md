## DIRECTION A — "Ribbon" (one long isometric landmass, the biomes as regions of one island)
Architecture: the five biome slabs of the reference are FUSED into ONE long, continuous isometric landmass — a single raised island, three to four screens wide, on the dark water table, seen from the reference's isometric angle. There are no slab edges between regions: the ground plane is continuous and the terrain MORPHS along it — corrugated steel and container stacks (INDUSTRIAL, IRONWORKS and VORTEX OIL signage, sodium lamps) crumble into red sandstone mesas and a campfire (CANYON), the sandstone rises into snow with pines, a frozen chairlift and a lit cabin (SNOW), the snow runs down into neon rooftops (NIGHT CITY, KESTREL TYRES sign) and the rooftops give onto black iron and molten pools (FOUNDRY, BOLT ENERGY, APEX SUSPENSION) at the far end. Each seam is a real blend at least half a screen wide (rusted rail bridges into the canyon; snow melting into the canyon's shade; the city's outer streets lit by the foundry's glow). The island's cut front face shows the strata changing colour with the biomes and carries the region name + count engraved once per region: "INDUSTRIAL · 3 / 4", "CANYON · 3 / 3", "SNOW · 0 / 2", "NIGHT CITY · 0 / 2", "FOUNDRY · 0 / 4".
