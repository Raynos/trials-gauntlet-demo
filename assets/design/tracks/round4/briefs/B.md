## DIRECTION B — "Chart" (a cartographic world map: dark-ops topographic chart, the diorama tiles as its terrain)
Architecture: the world is drawn as ONE MAP SHEET — a dark-ops topographic chart in the Broadcast palette (charcoal paper #0e1014, fine amber-grey contour lines, a faint grid with grid letters A–H across the top and 1–4 down the side, a compass rose, a scale bar "0 — 500 m", a map title block "TRIALS GAUNTLET · WORLD MAP · SHEET 1 / 1" in the display face) — but the FIVE BIOMES are not flat map fills: they are the reference's isometric night-lit diorama terrain SET INTO the chart like relief models, one per region, their edges feathering into the contour paper rather than cut square, and the regions run into each other along the map (industrial docklands at the west, canyon, snow massif in the centre-north, night city south-east, foundry at the east edge; a coastline runs along the south with the dark water table). Contour lines continue across the seams so the map reads as one land. Each region's name is lettered on the paper in tracked-out map capitals with its count: "INDUSTRIAL · 3 / 4", "CANYON · 3 / 3", "SNOW · 0 / 2", "NIGHT CITY · 0 / 2", "FOUNDRY · 0 / 4", with small pictographic map symbols (a crane, a mesa, a peak, a tower, a furnace).
The ROUTE is a DASHED EXPEDITION LINE inked on the chart, threading every track in tier order, with small arrowheads: solid AMBER ink from B1 through B3 and E1 through E3 (cleared), amber dashes to M1 ("UP NEXT" flag), thin grey dashes on through M2 and M3, and where it crosses into Night City a BARRIER drawn as a red-and-white striped map barrier symbol across the line with a label box "HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK · NEXT UNLOCK: H1 ROOFTOP WIRE"; the Extreme legs to X1, X2, X3 are grey dashes crossed by a second barrier "EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK". Locked regions are hatched with the classic diagonal map hatch.
Tracks are MAP MARKERS: the reference's 44 px round pin head (code + medal disc) on a short tail, planted on the relief terrain, with a small label box under each (name, PB / target; "MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK" on locked ones; H1 lit "NEXT UNLOCK"). Medal discs as before; padlocks on locked heads.
The LAB and the PLAYGROUNDS are on the same sheet as a different kind of symbol: the NORDVIK hangar drawn as a small isometric shed model at the map's south-west corner labelled "LAB · PROVING GROUND · NO MEDALS" (pin "LAB · PHYSICS TEST"), and the five playgrounds as small triangular PENNANT flags planted at the edge of their regions: "P1 CONTAINER YARD · OPEN", "P2 CANYON RUN · OPEN", "P3 SNOW LINE · OPEN", "P4 NIGHT CIRCUIT · OPEN", "P5 FOUNDRY FLOOR · OPEN".
PAN: one axis, LEFT / RIGHT along the sheet; a swipe pans, the minimap strip's bracket follows, tapping the strip flies. The chart is about 2.5 screens wide (a wider camera than direction A, because markers are smaller than diorama pins — about two biomes on screen). No pages, no dots, no arrows.
FRAMING: the west two-fifths of the sheet — the Lab shed and the P1 pennant at the bottom-left, INDUSTRIAL with B1 (gold, focused, amber ring; its card rising from the marker: "B1 · BEGINNER · INDUSTRIAL / FIRST RIDE / Best 0:41.200 · Target 1:05.000 / ▶ GHOST · 2 / 6 NOTED"), B2 (silver, LEAN BACK, 0:47.900), B3 (bronze, KICKER ROW, 0:58.100), M1 (dashed, HOP UP, "UP NEXT"); CANYON with E1 (silver, UPHILL WEIGHT, 0:52.400), E2 (bronze, REAR WHEEL FIRST, 1:06.800), E3 (silver, STAIRWAY, 1:01.300, PRO chip) and the P2 pennant; the SNOW massif's lower slopes at the right with M2 (dashed, DRUM ROLL) and, at the right edge, the HARD barrier symbol with its label box, the hatched Night City corner just visible beyond it.
MINIMAP STRIP directly above the bottom band, full width, drawn as the sheet's own INDEX MAP (a thumbnail of the whole chart with the five region colours, the coastline, 22 medal dots, padlocks on the hatched stretches, and an amber rectangle for the camera's window over the left ~40 %).
Header, bottom band (RIDE FIRST RIDE ▸ bottom-right, 6 / 15 CLEARED bottom-left) and ticker as in the shared brief; the map title block sits under the header at the top-left of the sheet and must not collide with the badge plate.
