# World map — three from-scratch directions (mockups, no decision)

Round subject: the user, on the level select that is live now (round 5, `docs/evidence/level-select/round5/before-after-far-932x430.jpg`; ask 53 in `docs/tasks/ASKS.md`): *"What the fuck is that world map? It's just dioramas and a link on like one page. Use Codex CLI, make three new mockups of a beautiful world map. Like Final Fantasy VII — the second Final Fantasy VII remake [Rebirth]. A beautiful world map like Final Fantasy XV's world map. Beautiful world map like Final Fantasy XVI's world map. Beautiful world map like the world map in Black Desert Online. Your world map looks like you just refactored the diorama to show on one page and connected those floating squares together. It's complete shit. It needs to be from scratch, built absolutely from scratch all the way up. A brand new world map."*

So this round breaks lineage on purpose: **none of the three derives from the diorama tiles, the pins-on-posts, the slab plates or the isometric-cut-tile language.** Each is a single continuous landscape — one continent — that pans in two axes and zooms, with the 15 campaign tracks, the Lab and the five playgrounds as places on it. The Broadcast chrome survives only as a thin layer (badge plate + build stamp, `‹ MENU`, `6 / 15 CLEARED` + medal dots, a RIDE pill). **No game code changed and nothing is decided here — the user picks.**

- Contact sheet (the rejected live far view + A / B / C at region zoom + A / B / C whole world + the three kept extra runs, labelled): `assets/design/worldmap/contact-sheet.jpg`
- Directions, full-res PNG as generated (1536×1024 with the letterbox) and the same cropped to JPG: `A-painted-region`, `A-painted-world`, `B-sheet-region`, `B-sheet-world`, `C-nodes-region`, `C-nodes-world` (`.png` / `.jpg`). Kept extra runs (§ 4): `B-sheet-world-run2-wide`, `C-nodes-region-run1-tall`, `C-nodes-world-run1-tall`.
- The rejected screen, for contrast: `docs/evidence/level-select/round5/far-chromium-932x430.jpg` (the live build's zoomed-out view, captured headless at 932×430) — six isometric plates chained diagonally over the garage backdrop, a region list down the right. It is the first tile of the contact sheet so the user sees exactly what these replace.
- `build/` in this folder is **not** part of the round: it is the production plate pipeline of `docs/plans/WORLD_MAP.md` (ask 54 — the user's pick, A, being built from scratch), which started while this round was closing.
- Briefs, so the parent can rerun: `briefs/shared.md` (the framing, the geography, the 21 places and their seeded state, the thin chrome — and an explicit list of what the map must NOT be), `briefs/{A,B,C}.md` (style only), `briefs/{region,world}.md` (the viewport), `briefs/retry.md` (a stronger frame note, appended on a retry), `briefs/gen.sh <A|B|C> <region|world> [logdir] [retry]`, `briefs/sheet.py`, `briefs/logs/` (codex's final note per run + `summary.txt` with the timings). Recipe: `cat briefs/shared.md briefs/<X>.md briefs/<view>.md | codex exec -s workspace-write -o <log> -` from the repo root with the output path appended as the last line — **no `-i` reference at all**: the live level select must not anchor the model on its tiles, and the only bike capture on disk carries a real brand, so the position marker is described in words instead.
- Timings. Six runs in parallel, wall clock **201 s**: A-region 125 s, C-region 132 s, B-region 184 s, A-world 199 s, B-world 200 s, C-world 201 s. Three retries in parallel (§ 4), wall clock **199 s**: C-region 107 s, B-world 138 s, C-world 198 s. Total 6.7 min of generation.
- Every mockup shows the same seeded state as rounds 1–5: 6 / 15 cleared (all Beginner + Easy, E3 on Pro), M1 `UP NEXT`, H1 the next unlock (locked, rule "medal every Medium track") in Night City, B1 focused with `First Ride · 0:41.200 / 1:05.000` and `▶ GHOST`, locked regions gated. Build stamp `build fb947d4 · 2026-09-17` (HEAD at generation time).

They are direction studies, not pixel truth: the generator's geography differs slightly between the region and world images of the same direction (the river runs elsewhere, the canyon is bigger or smaller) — a real build has one terrain and both views are the same plate at two zooms.

## 1. Shared content, so the three compare on style alone

One continent, the route from the south-west coast to the far north-east through the five biomes in campaign order: **Industrial** (port and steelworks — rust, steel, gantry cranes, sodium lamps; holds LAB, P1, B1 B2 B3, M1 at its north edge) → across a river to **Canyon** (red sandstone at sunset; P2, E1 E2 E3) → **Snow** (ice-blue alps; P3, M2, X1 at the summit) → **Night City** on a bay (neon, wet streets; P4, H1, H2) → **Foundry** (molten orange on black iron; P5, M3, H3, X2, X3). Cleared places carry their medal colour; M1 M2 M3 are lit but medal-less; locked places are dim with a padlock and the locked *regions* are gated in each style; the route is solid amber B1 … E3, amber-dashed to M1, faint beyond. The "region" image is the view the screen opens on (Industrial large, Canyon beyond it, the rest off screen); the "world" image is the pinch-out (the whole continent, all 21 places, both gates, H1's rule readable). Locked-tier gating is therefore only *hinted* in the region images (haze / hatch at the frame edge) and *stated* in the world images — a real build shows the HARD gate as soon as the pan reaches the Snow / Night City edge.

## 3. Per direction

### A — "Painted terrain" (`A-painted-region.jpg`, `A-painted-world.jpg`)

**What it is.** A hand-painted terrain model seen from a high camera pitched ~40°: the port and steelworks on the coast with the LAB hangar on its apron and the P1 compound, a river with a rail bridge into the red mesas of the Canyon, and — in the world view — the snow range with a lit cabin and a chairlift, Night City on its bay under a cold fog with the neon bleeding through, and the Foundry smouldering on the far headland with an `EXTREME · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK` plate at its gate. Region names are large, elegant, tracked serif capitals that float over the land (`INDUSTRIAL 3 / 4`, `CANYON 3 / 3`, `SNOW 0 / 2`, `NIGHT CITY 0 / 2`, `FOUNDRY 0 / 4`). Every place is a small glowing diamond with a leader line to a dark name plate (`B2 · Lean Back`, `E3 · Stairway PRO`, `M1 · Hop Up UP NEXT`, `H1 · Rooftop Wire / NEXT UNLOCK · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`); B1's is the full plate (`B1 · BEGINNER · INDUSTRIAL / First Ride / 0:41.200 / 1:05.000 / ▶ GHOST`) with a tall amber beacon and the blue bike marker at its base. The route is a thin amber thread laid on the roads, dashed to M1; cloud shadows and mist sit on the land; the horizon is hazed.

**What it does with the brief.** It answers the ask most directly — this is the "region map with elevation and haze" language the user named first, and it is the only one of the three whose *map* has weather and time of day, so it can be alive (drifting cloud shadows, the beacon, the neon flicker in the fog) without any UI motion. The leader-line plates keep the labels off the terrain, which is what lets the land stay painterly. The world view is the strongest single image of the round: the five biomes read as one place with real geography between them (the river, the alpine lake, the bay), not five colours side by side.

**Mockup vs brief.** Region frame 1536×785 (1.96:1; read ~10 % taller than the phone), world 1536×736 (2.09:1). Deviations: P1 / P2 / the Lab are drawn as **gold** diamonds (playgrounds carry no medal — should be a neutral white/cyan; the world view gets this right with cyan); in the world view M2 and M3 are gold (uncleared — should be white-amber); `IRONWORKS` on the tower is garbled; the HARD gate in the region view is only the chevrons + a small plate at the top-right edge (the fog itself is off frame — correct for the zoom, but the plate is small). Complete otherwise: all 21 places in the world view, both gates, H1's rule, the chip, the pills.

**844×390.** The map band loses ~40 px of height; a tilted-terrain map simply shows a little less land — no layout breaks. The leader-line plates are the risk: at 844 four plates sit in Industrial and the generator already leans them apart; the build needs the plate-collision rule from round 3 (code + diamond only when unfocused, the plate on the focused place and on hover/drag-stop). The beacon and the pills are fine.

### B — "Cartographer's sheet" (`B-sheet-region.jpg`, `B-sheet-world.jpg`; run 2 `B-sheet-world-run2-wide.jpg`)

**What it is.** An illustrated map sheet seen straight down: ink coastlines with stippled shallows, hand-drawn mountain hatching, rivers, sea names in spaced italics (`GRAYWATER BAY`, `THE GAUNTLET SEA`, `COPPER RIVER`; the world view invents `STORMREACH SEA`, `CALDERA SEA`, `EMBERGULF`), a title cartouche `TRIALS GAUNTLET · THE GAUNTLET COAST`, a compass rose and a `0 – 20 KM` scale bar. Each region has an engraved cartouche with its name and count (Industrial's carries a motto line `STEEL · LABOUR · PROGRESS`; Canyon's `RED ROCK · ENDURANCE · HIGHER`). Places are round ink seals with a painted vignette inside (a gantry crane for B1, a stairway for E3, a rooftop for H1) and the code + name beside; the cleared seals in the world view carry their best / target in green. The route is a flowing amber line with arrowheads, dashed to M1; the locked regions are diagonally hatched and lettered `UNCHARTED`, with an engraved barrier symbol across the road at the Night City edge (`HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK`) and a second at the Foundry (`EXTREME GATE · MEDAL EVERY HARD TRACK`). The B1 plate is a legend box; the bike marker is a tiny ink motorbike.

**What it does with the brief.** It is the "world map as a document" — the cartographic language the user named second and third — and the only direction where every piece of information is *of the map*: cartouches, seals, a route line, hatching, a compass. That makes it the most legible of the three at every zoom (flat, no perspective, no horizon; text never fights terrain), the cheapest to make consistent between region and world views (one flat plate scales), and the most distinctive against every other Trials-like game. The cost is that it is the least "alive": the sheet does not have weather, and the vignette seals are the whole personality of a place.

**Mockup vs brief.** The generator drifted from the briefed **dark-slate paper with cream ink** to a fully painted, lit sheet — the region view is closer to a painted-relief map than to ink; the world view keeps the dark paper at the edges and the hatch. Both are more colourful than briefed and better for it; if the user picks B, the brief's "dark slate + cream ink" line should be softened to "painted sheet on dark paper". Region frame 1536×746 (2.06:1 — the best-framed of the six). World run 1 is 1536×824 (1.86:1, taller than the phone: the sheet's grid would show ~15 % less at 19.5:9); it also draws a **full row of six sponsor logos** across the bottom band (the brief said sparingly — one billboard) and puts the RIDE pill right of GHOST (correct) but the row pushes both up. The retry (`run2-wide`) came back on an **1846×852 canvas, 2.79:1** — codex's own note: *"The tool returned 1846×852, so the requested dimensions and letterboxing were not met"* — but it is the best drawing of a B world (the barrier signpost across the road, the route flowing round the coast, only one sponsor), so it is kept and labelled; run 1 is canonical for the frame. Text: `1:06.800` for E2 in green in the world view (over target — should be white); the region view's M1 `UP NEXT` route leg runs M1 → E1 dashed rather than E3 → M1.

**844×390.** The best of the three: a flat sheet at 844 shows 9 % less paper, nothing else moves; the seals are 44 px discs with the label beside, so four in Industrial fit at region zoom. The title cartouche and scale bar are decoration and can drop at 844; the compass stays (it is 60 px in the corner under the CLEARED chip).

### C — "Satellite nodes" (`C-nodes-region.jpg`, `C-nodes-world.jpg`; run 1 `C-nodes-region-run1-tall.jpg`, `C-nodes-world-run1-tall.jpg`)

**What it is.** A painterly aerial photograph: the port grid with container stacks and orange cranes, the LAB hangar on the quay, the river and the red-rock canyon, and in the world view the alps, the neon city and the lava-veined foundry, all lit as one dusk-to-night image. Over it the node network: round dark discs with a pictogram (a flask for LAB, a box for P1, a ramp for B3, a mountain for E1) and a medal-colour ring, joined by glowing amber links along the roads (dashed to M1, grey under the fog); a dashed territory border round Industrial with `INDUSTRIAL 3 / 4` in bold condensed capitals. The locked regions sit under a desaturated fog with an amber `HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK` banner at the crossing; H1's node is the lit padlock inside it. The right third is the **info panel**: a painted thumbnail of the track (the bike on a steel ramp under the IRONWORKS sign), `B1 · BEGINNER · INDUSTRIAL / First Ride`, the gold medal, `Best 0:41.200 · Target 1:05.000`, `▶ GHOST`, and the amber `RIDE FIRST RIDE ▸` pill at its bottom; the world view's panel adds a table of the other cleared times.

**What it does with the brief.** It is the MMO node-map the user named fourth: discovery as a network, with the terrain as a backdrop and a panel doing the talking. It is the only direction that gives the *track* a picture (the thumbnail — which the garage/hero pipeline can already produce per biome), and the panel is where a review count, leaderboard and ghost list would go without touching the map. The trade is real estate: the panel takes ~300 of 932 px on a phone, so the map is a 630 px window at region zoom, and the network + territory language is the most "UI" of the three — the terrain is beautiful but it is behind the graph.

**Mockup vs brief.** Run 1 of both views came back too tall (region 1.77:1, world 1.69:1 with a grey-gradient letterbox, not black) and the region run lacked the territory tint and P2; the retries with `retry.md` fixed the frame (2.06:1 / 2.04:1), added the territory border and P2, and are canonical. Deviations that remain: territories are outlined, not tinted; the world view again draws a six-logo sponsor row; E1 / E2 / E3 rings are silver / gold / silver in the world view (E2 should be bronze); B1's on-map plate is duplicated by the panel (in a build the on-map plate would collapse to the code + name when the panel is open). The fog of war over Night City is light — the neon reads through it more than a "can't go here" fog should.

**844×390.** The worst of the three: the panel is full-height and ~300 px wide, so at 844 the map window is ~544×390 — one region, barely. The build would need the panel to collapse to a bottom sheet (thumbnail + name + RIDE, ~120 px tall) at 844, which is a second layout to design and test.

## 4. Runs, retries and what is kept

Six first runs, then one retry each for the three furthest from 19.5:9 (C-region 1.77, C-world 1.69, B-world 1.86), with `briefs/retry.md` appended (one paragraph restating the 1536×708 frame and pure-black letterbox). Both C retries hit ~2.05:1 and are canonical; the B-world retry overshot to a 1846×852, 2.79:1 canvas and is kept as `-run2-wide` with run 1 canonical. As in round 4, both runs of every retried image are on disk and on the contact sheet. No run garbled the seeded state; the recurring generator errors are medal colours on no-medal places and the sponsor row (twice) where one billboard was asked.

## 5. From-scratch build estimate per direction

Common to all three — **a new `WorldMapScreen`** replacing `TrackSelectScreen` (`src/ui/front.ts:420–830`, 1619 lines total in the file) and its data module `src/ui/trackMap.ts` (397 lines: six pages, plate boxes, `tile-<page>.webp`); the six tile plates, five seam strips, massif and three dressing sprites in `public/art/tiles/` (1.5 MB) are **retired, not reused**. Kept as-is: the drag-with-inertia and pinch-about-pointer gesture code already in `front.ts:430–470` (it is the only part of the current screen that maps 1:1), the seeded save / `nextTrack()` / lastTrack rules, the RIDE / GHOST / REVIEW actions, the fonts (Barlow Condensed — `Trials Display` 900 italic for names, `Trials UI` 500 / 700 for labels; both already loaded), and the harness stills / clip scripts (re-pointed at the new screen). One world-space coordinate for every place (`{x, y}` on the plate in plate px) replaces today's per-page pin boxes. The art pipeline is the codex `image_gen` → cut / tile → WebP path used for the tiles and the garage plates, plus a new tiling step for a plate larger than 1024 px.

| | A — Painted terrain | B — Cartographer's sheet | C — Satellite nodes |

## 6. Recommendation (the user picks)

**B, the cartographer's sheet, with A's beacon and fog borrowed for the locked edge.** It is the direction that most completely stops looking like the thing the user rejected (nothing in it is a plate, a pin or a tile; it is a drawing of a place), it is the most legible at region *and* world zoom because it is flat, it is the cheapest to build and the safest on iOS Safari (one flat plate, two tiers, CSS hatch), and it survives 844×390 without a second layout. Its weakness — it is a still document — is answered by taking A's two live elements: the amber beacon on the focused place and a slow drift in the `UNCHARTED` haze. **A** is the more spectacular picture and the one to pick if the world view on the contact sheet is the feeling the user wants at any cost (~one extra round and the tier-swap perf work). **C** is the right shape only if the panel — thumbnails, times table, leaderboards — is wanted as the level select's main surface; on a phone it halves the map.

Nothing here is decided; the round is three pictures and a cost.
