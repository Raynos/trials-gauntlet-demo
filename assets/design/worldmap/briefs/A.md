
## Direction A — "Painted terrain" (a tilted 3-D painterly region map)
The map is a painterly, slightly tilted 3-D model of the continent, seen from a high camera pitched about 35–45 degrees, so the land has REAL ELEVATION: mountains rise, canyons cut down, the coast drops to the sea, and the horizon is visible along the top of the frame with a soft atmospheric haze and a gentle depth-of-field blur on the far land. Think of a hand-painted terrain model: thick painterly forests, ochre and rust plains, sandstone mesas, snow with blue shadows, a neon-dotted city on a bay, a smouldering black foundry in the distance. Soft cloud shadows drift across the land. Roads and rails are drawn as thin real roads on the terrain, not as UI lines. Water is dark and reflective with a faint animated sheen.
- Region names are set in LARGE, elegant, widely-tracked serif capitals floating just above the terrain of each region, slightly transparent, with the count in a small line beneath: "INDUSTRIAL  3 / 4", "CANYON  3 / 3", "SNOW  0 / 2", "NIGHT CITY  0 / 2", "FOUNDRY  0 / 4". The Lab is labelled "LAB · PROVING GROUND".
- Places are small GLOWING ICONS standing on the terrain (a small diamond / spire of light in the medal colour; a padlock glyph for locked ones), each with a thin LEADER LINE up to a tiny name plate reading the code and the name (e.g. "B2 · Lean Back"). The focused B1 has a tall vertical amber BEACON of light rising from it into the sky, its full name plate as described, and the rider marker at its base.
- The route is a thin glowing thread laid on the roads: amber where cleared, dashed to M1, and beyond it dimmed under the haze.
- GATING: the locked regions (Night City, Foundry, the Snow summit) are sunk in a cold blue-grey ATMOSPHERIC FOG — the land is still visible as silhouette and the city's neon glows through the murk — with a faint row of amber hazard chevrons where the route crosses into the fog and a small plate "HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK" on that crossing. H1 is the one lit point in the fog, with its "NEXT UNLOCK" label.
- Colour: dusk-to-night lighting from the south-west (warm) to the north-east (cold, neon, then molten). Painterly, luminous, high dynamic range; the UI chrome is crisp on top.
