
## Direction B — "Cartographer's sheet" (a top-down illustrated map sheet)
The map is a beautiful hand-drawn CARTOGRAPHIC SHEET seen straight from above: the continent inked on dark slate-blue paper (#151a22) with fine cream and gold linework, the way a prestige fantasy game draws its world map. Coastlines in confident ink with a double line and stippled shallows; relief shown by hand-drawn MOUNTAIN HATCHING and soft painted shading; rivers as thin blue-white lines; forests as small clustered tree glyphs; the desert as stippled sand; the city as a dense block plan with tiny lit windows; the foundry as cross-hatched black with orange glow bleeding through the paper. Roads are fine dashed ink lines with tiny mile ticks. The paper has subtle age — faint fibre, a few ink-flecks — but the type is crisp.
- Every region carries an ORNATE CARTOUCHE (a small engraved frame with flourishes) holding its name in engraved serif capitals and the count beneath: "INDUSTRIAL · 3 / 4", "CANYON · 3 / 3", "SNOW · 0 / 2", "NIGHT CITY · 0 / 2", "FOUNDRY · 0 / 4". Region areas are subtly TINTED (rust, red-sand, ice, violet, ember) with a soft-edged wash. A large COMPASS ROSE in the sea at the lower-left and a scale bar; a small title cartouche "TRIALS GAUNTLET · THE GAUNTLET COAST" along the sea at the top edge.
- Places are small LOCATION ICONS: a circular ink-drawn seal (medal colour ring, a padlock inside for locked places) with a tiny PAINTED VIGNETTE of the place inside it (a crane, a mesa, a chairlift, a rooftop, a furnace) and the code and name lettered beside it in small engraved caps (e.g. "E3 · Stairway  PRO"). The focused B1 has an amber ring, a small amber wax-seal glow, its name plate as described drawn like a map legend box, and the rider marker (a tiny ink-drawn motorbike) beside it.
- The route is a ROUTE LINE between discovered places: solid amber ink where cleared, amber-dashed to M1, and beyond that a faint dotted grey line of "uncharted" travel.
- GATING: locked regions are DIAGONALLY HATCHED in fine grey ink with a soft dark wash and the legend "UNCHARTED"; a small engraved barrier symbol crosses the road where it enters Night City with the label "HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK". H1's seal is drawn a little brighter than the other locked seals with its "NEXT UNLOCK" line.
- Colour: dark slate paper, cream ink, gold highlights, the five region tints; the UI chrome (translucent charcoal, amber) sits on top like instruments on a chart table.
