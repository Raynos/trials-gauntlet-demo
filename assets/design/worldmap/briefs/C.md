
## Direction C — "Satellite nodes" (a rich painted terrain from above with a node network)
The map is a rich, full-colour PAINTERLY SATELLITE VIEW of the continent from straight above, like a high-resolution aerial painting: photoreal-painterly terrain textures — the rust-and-concrete grid of the port, the red rock and dry washes of the canyon, the white ridges and blue shadow of the alps, the glittering grid of the city at night, the black ash and orange lava-veins of the foundry — with real rivers, roads, coastline and cloud wisps, lit as a single dusk-to-night image. The terrain is continuous and detailed at every zoom.
- Over the terrain sits a NODE NETWORK: every place is a round NODE ICON (a dark disc with a thin bright ring in the medal colour and a small pictogram inside; a padlock for locked), and nodes are joined by GLOWING ROUTE LINKS along the roads. Discovered / cleared nodes and links are lit (amber links, medal-coloured rings); the open-uncleared nodes M1 M2 M3 glow white-amber; locked nodes and links are dim. Each node has a small label beside it with the code and name in clean sans caps (e.g. "B3 · KICKER ROW").
- TERRITORIES: each region is tinted by a translucent coloured territory overlay with a soft glowing border line (rust, red, ice-blue, violet, ember), and its name is set in bold sans capitals with the count under it near the region's centre: "INDUSTRIAL  3 / 4" and so on for the five regions; "LAB" small at the proving ground.
- GATING: a FOG OF WAR — a desaturated, darkened, slightly blurred haze — covers the locked regions (Night City, Foundry, the Snow summit); the terrain is barely legible under it, the nodes there are grey with padlocks, and at the fog's edge where the route crosses into Night City a small amber banner reads "HARD · MEDAL EVERY MEDIUM TRACK". H1's node is the one lit point inside the fog, labelled "NEXT UNLOCK".
- RIGHT-SIDE INFO PANEL: because B1 is selected, a translucent dark panel (about 300 screen px wide, full height minus the top and bottom chrome) sits on the right edge with a PAINTED THUMBNAIL of the track (an industrial yard at night with a motorbike on a steel ramp) at the top, then "B1 · BEGINNER · INDUSTRIAL", "First Ride" large, a gold medal, "Best 0:41.200 · Target 1:05.000", "▶ GHOST", and the "RIDE  FIRST RIDE ▸" pill at the panel's bottom (this replaces the free-floating RIDE pill in the corner). The panel never covers the focused node.
- The rider marker (a small blue-and-white motorbike icon on a round base) stands on B1's node, which has an amber selection ring and a pulsing amber halo.
- Colour: saturated, luminous painterly terrain; the network in amber and white; the fog cold grey; the chrome and panel translucent charcoal.
