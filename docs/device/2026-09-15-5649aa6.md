## What it decides

1. **PERF rung 1 is met: the garage is a flat 30** (was 24–28 with 66 ms worst before `0f14d3d` + `cfc98f8`).
   The fixed cost was the free-running cap (a lost render slot per overrun) plus the CSS on the canvas.
2. **60 fps on `low` holds on this phone**: 59.5 fps, interval p95 17 ms, 6 drops in 1 191 frames, JS 2.1 ms.
   → the phone cap moves 30 → 60 in Auto (the RIDER_ON_GLASS G3 rule: "only when the device report says so").
3. **The JS frame at cap 30 is not the JS frame**: `submit` reads 4.7–5.3 ms at cap 30 and 1.86 ms at cap 60 on
   the same scene — at 30 the driver back-pressures inside `renderer.render` (or the CPU clocks down). The
   headless "0.8 ms CPU" is right; the phone's cost sits in the GPU/driver, which the bench cannot see but the
   cap-60 row bounds: low fits with ~14 ms to spare.
4. **Medium and high at 60 are unmeasured.** Medium at 30 holds with a 6.9 ms JS frame and 2.08 Mpx of HDR +
   bloom + a 1024² shadow; high (6.57 Mpx, 2048² shadow, SSAO) already shows p95 47 ms at 30 — it does not hold
   30 on this phone. Next report: `?bench=1&cap=60` after the cap flip (adds medium/high at 60 rows).
5. **Cold first entry: `entryMs` 23 177 ms** on the menu row (the boot's backdrop world: shader link on Metal +
   760 ms texture gen); warm re-entry 616 ms. Programs accumulate across tiers (`stalePrograms` 12 → 85 →
   72) and are never released since the r14 prune removal — a real dispose path is a PERF item (Astra's branch
   claims "full shader-program reclamation"; a merge candidate).
