# Ask 52: "the in-game bike looks completely different from the prototype's"

Measured, the shipped bike is the delivered bike. Two matched comparisons, both liveries:

1. **three.js, neutral studio** (`viewer.html` + `shoot.mjs`: GLTFLoader + Meshopt, RoomEnvironment PMREM, one sun, ACES, sRGB out, SwiftShader Chromium at 1280x720; same camera for both files): `rookie-pair-grid.png` / `pro-pair-grid.png` = delivered (left) vs shipped (right), three-quarter front (top) and tank/fender close-up (bottom). Per-part mean rendered colour (each part isolated, background masked; `*-parts.json`): largest difference over 12 parts is 0.034 in 0..1 sRGB (bodywork: 0.517/0.594/0.819 vs 0.495/0.573/0.804 rookie), forks 0.008, swingarm 0.006, exhaust 0.007, pegs 0.002. Decals (VORTEX, NORDVIK, plate 7), gold fork uppers, satin frame, dark engine and the clearcoat fender all survive the atlas bake; the delivered file has 16 materials, the shipped 4 (atlas, chain, blur card x2).
2. **The game garage itself** (render owner's `docs/evidence/hero-art/pose-compare/still.mts`, headless, build 7573b06): `game-garage-delivered-vs-shipped.png` = the delivered `bike-rookie-art.glb` temporarily swapped in (left) vs the shipped `bike-rookie.glb` (right), same shot. They are the same picture: the pale rear fender, the saturated tank, the washed frame appear in both.

So the difference the user sees is between the **prototype's neutral studio** (`pose-compare/prototype-garage-sit-cruise.png`: grey walls, soft white light, no grade) and the **game garage's lighting and grade** (warm key from the right, blue fill, wet-floor reflections, bloom, biome grade, envMapIntensity 0.8): the dark-grey rear fender reads near white under the key, the blue tank saturates, the steel-blue frame reads white-blue. That is the garage stage / grade, not the asset; a material-side change (clearcoat physical materials, texture transforms, 2048 decals) would not move it, as comparison 2 shows. Recommendation to the render owner: a neutral "studio" garage light preset or a lower key/bloom for the hero, judged against the prototype still. No `public/models` change was made for this ask; budgets unchanged (23 draws, 33,204 tris, 2048/1024 images).
