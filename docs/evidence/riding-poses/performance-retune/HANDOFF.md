## Retained implementation

- `riderRigFromCOM` reuses the complete forward geometry already present for its converged best iterate. A failed/backtracked solve still reconstructs its best earlier iterate. The fixed initial guess and 10nm squared-error convergence bar remain.
- `solveHold` caches deterministic torso sin/cos by the complete angle key. This is pure memoization; a foreign restored angle selects its own exact result.
- During the velocity solver, each of the five hold rows' C/gx/gy/ga values is computed on iteration zero and reused in later iterations. Positions, angles and tuning do not change within that velocity loop. Every solve starts by overwriting the rows, including an Infinity sentinel for skipped rows. The position projection pass recomputes its rows as before. Therefore no row cache is read across a tick, restore, load or restart.
