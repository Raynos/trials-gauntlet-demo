## Verdict: **CLEAR WITH CAVEATS**

- No live ROCKHOP or ROCK HOP trademark was found in any office TMview covers, including the USPTO. No app on either store and no game on Steam uses the exact name.
- **Caveat 1, near-name games:** "Rockhopper" and "Rock Hopper" are used by several small 2024 indie games on iOS, Play and Steam, but none are bike games. Store search for "rockhop" will show them. Keep the name as one word, "Rockhop", with no "-per".
- **Caveat 2, domains and handles:** rockhop.com, .net and .app and YouTube @rockhop are taken. Use **rockhop.gg** or **rockhopgame.com** (both unregistered today; register before the announcement) and the handle **@rockhopgame** everywhere.
- **Caveat 3, same-name business:** Rockhop (rockhop.ai) is a B2B Microsoft consultancy with no registered mark found. It is a different market, so confusion is unlikely, but it will compete with the game for the plain "Rockhop" search result.
- **Caveat 4, ROCKHOPPER marks in IC 41:** the Lewis Silkin (EU/UK) and Rockhopper Running marks cover the "-hopper" form and unrelated services. Low risk for "Rockhop" in IC 9/41, but do not use "Rockhopper" as a mode or character name.
- Optional: file ROCKHOP in IC 9 and IC 41 (USPTO or UKIPO) after launch, if protection is wanted.
