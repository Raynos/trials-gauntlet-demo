## What it is

One tilted painted continent in campaign order: Industrial docks + refinery bottom-left (the Lab hangar and P1 on the pier), the Canyon mesas above across the river, the Snow peak top-centre with a chairlift, Night City on its bay across the long bridge, the Foundry burning on the far north-east shore under haze; waterfalls, harbour, lighthouse. Tracks are diamond markers standing on the terrain with leader-line name plates (blue = playground / Lab, gold / silver / bronze = medalled, white = open, padlock = locked); the campaign route is a lit road (only the legs out of medalled tracks lit); the hazard gate stands where the road enters the next locked tier; the current track carries the bike, a beacon and its card; `6 / 15 CLEARED` + medal dots bottom-left; RIDE + GHOST pills bottom-right; badge plate + build stamp top-left; `‹ MENU` top-right. Fog of war over locked regions; drifting cloud shadows; a horizon haze band.

## Architecture (from scratch — nothing of the diorama is reused)

1. **`src/ui/worldMap.ts`** — pure data + layout, unit-tested (`worldMap.test.ts`): the map's coordinate space (the world plate's pixel grid, 1536 × 1024), one anchor per track id, the five region bounds (the region plates' crops), the route polyline through every campaign track in tier order, the gate position (on the route, at the mouth of the next locked tier), the fog polygons per locked region, the zoom tiers (`fit` / `region` / `max`, the tier cross-fade threshold, the plate-hide zoom), `buildMarkers(tracks, medalOf, dev)`, `nextGate`, `routePath`, `markersInView`, `fitZoom`.
2. **`src/ui/worldMapScreen.ts`** — `WorldMapScreen`, the DOM screen: one `translate/scale` root (`.wm-scene`) under a `touch-action: none` viewport; the world plate `<img>` and five region plates cross-fading in above the tier threshold; an SVG route layer (dim + lit), the fog layer (soft alpha polygons), the cloud-shadow layer (`prefers-reduced-motion` off), the marker layer (buttons counter-scaled to a 44 px diamond at every zoom), the gate; screen-space chrome: badge + stamp, `‹ MENU`, the progress chip, the card, RIDE / GHOST. Camera: one-finger / mouse drag with inertia, pinch about the midpoint, wheel + Safari `gesturechange` about the pointer, tap vs drag by travel (8 px), 380 ms eased fly-to. Opens centred on the last played track (else `nextTrack()`) at region zoom. Keys / pad: `nav()` steps markers along the route (camera follows), `confirm` = RIDE, `back` = MENU, `alt` = GHOST. Same `FrontCallbacks` / `FrontState` / `bestOf` / `boardOf` reads as the old screen, so play / lock rules / best times are unchanged.
3. **Terrain art** — two zoom tiers of chrome-free painted terrain: the world plate (the A world view regenerated as a clean plate: same continent, same composition, no labels / markers / route glow / UI, roads as painted dirt / asphalt, 1536 × 1024 filling the frame) and five region plates (one per biome, generated with the world plate as reference plus a crop guide, ~3× the detail, feathered at the region boundaries; where a seam can't be made clean the region tier cross-fades to the world tier around it). Sources, briefs and logs under `assets/design/worldmap/build/`; cut through `assets/art/build.mjs` (manifest tiers 1x / 2x, webp stepped to byte caps) into `public/art/worldmap/`; all lazy (never on the boot set). Budget: world plate ≤ 350 KB per tier, region plates ≤ 300 KB each.
4. **App wiring** — `src/game/app.ts` swaps `TrackSelectScreen` for `WorldMapScreen` behind the same `'tracks'` screen id and `nav / confirm / back / alt` dispatch. Then the diorama retires: `TrackSelectScreen`, `src/ui/trackMap.ts`, `trackMap.test.ts`, `trackSelect.test.ts`, the `.tmap` block of `styles.ts`, `public/art/tiles/`, the six diorama plates + seams + sprites in the art pack (`assets/art/tiles.mjs`, their `selection.json` / manifest / `build.mjs` entries), `harness/e2e/tracks-{stills,clip,frames,climb-clip}.mts`.
5. **Phone first** — 932 × 430 and 844 × 390 with safe-area insets, every tappable ≥ 44 px, ≤ 2 taps to B1 once focused, a locked H1 states its rule on its plate, the card and the gate; desktop second. A handful of composited layers, no per-frame layout; frame time measured in the harness (30 fps on the user's iPhone is the bar the garage holds).
