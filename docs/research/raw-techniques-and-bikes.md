## B. Input model
- HD: Left Stick lean back/forward; RT accelerate; LT brake; B restart; Y bailout https://trials.fandom.com/wiki/Trials_HD
- Fusion: lean = left stick L/R; gas A or RT; brake X or LT; FMX pose right stick https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/718804-trials-fusion/faqs/69296
- Throttle analogue: "press it slightly, and the bike moves slow, press it all the way, and the bike roars."
- Lean on the ground effectively binary, analogue in the air: "leaning is simply a forward, backward response... but that's only for leaning the biker, in the air, you can determine how fast you can rotate the bike around by how far you push the stick." D-pad = digital lean, "the biker leans quicker".
- Rising quantisation: LT 3 brake levels, RT 7 throttle levels; Switch throttle on right stick (no analogue triggers) — Kaiser Panda.
- Rider has 5 effective positions (Kaiser Panda): hard-back (stick held), light-back (released from back), neutral, light-forward, hard-forward; holding full forward puts all weight on the front and risks lifting the rear; released-forward keeps the rear down.
- Keyboard: Up gas, Down brake, Right lean fwd, Left lean back; "one of the best riders is playing only with keyboard" https://steamcommunity.com/app/245490/discussions/0/618453594754817540/
- Brake is a single input (no front/rear split in any source); long-press brake = reverse. Bail = Y/Triangle.

## C. Techniques

### C1 Bunny hop
- Lesson 7 (RedLynx, FatShady) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-a_ulossbyc: "consider that your rear suspension is a spring, and the more pressure you apply, the harder it bounces back. This release of pressure and shifting your body weight will make the bike jump." / "As you start accelerating, sit back on the bike and load the suspension to build up potential energy... when it's at its maximum, release the pressure by leaning forward. The timing of these two movements is crucial." / "Lean back to load the suspension, lean forward to release the pressure, and then quickly lean back again. What you'll see is that this final step will lift the bike underneath you and ensure that the back wheel doesn't hit anything."
- FatShady detailed https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28QIiX-jlmw: "if you just start from a seated position and try and do a bunny hop it's not physically possible... to generate spring for a bunny hop you either need to be standing or leaning forward" / "as you accelerate... lean back... sit down over the bike and that will build up the energy in the springs... just at the point where you've got maximum load on those Springs you then want to push yourself forward" / "you're not only releasing the potential energy from the rear springs... you're actually pulling the bike" / "a pretty quick and succinct left right and then potentially left again movement" / "you usually finish a bunny hop by then seating yourself back down again and... that will then lift the rear wheel up to your new height" / "you are waiting until the maximum load is over those back Springs and then releasing that pressure".
- Antti Ilvessuo (RedLynx): "Lean forward… gas… lean back really fast and then forward, to get the springs loaded and then release that momentum at the right time." https://www.videogamer.com/features/trials-evolution-tips-and-advice-from-redlynx/
- "You want to get your front wheel up high while still having it remain in front of you (not above) and then thrust forward" https://steamcommunity.com/app/245490/discussions/0/558754898993513386/
- Kaiser Panda: [Get the front wheel up a little (light-back), then immediately press back then forward quickly. Depending on how much the front wheel is up you choose: higher or further.]
- Trials 2 SE dev wiki https://trials.fandom.com/wiki/Trials_2:_Second_Edition: "Switch Jump: Doable when you are leaning fully forwards... Quickly tap lean back and then immediately tap lean forward just before your front tire goes over the ramp edge. When at up hill press lean back and lean forward very shortly or you will likely flip... When downhill press the buttons for a longer time to get even higher... It doesn't help with horizontal jump distance (often actually making your jump shorter)." / "Spring Jump: Doable when you are leaning fully backwards... Just before your front wheel goes over the ramp edge, press forward and keep it pressed until the driver is fully at front position and your backwheel is in air. The main usage of Spring Jump is to get further..." / "Spring Jump Reverse: ... immediately when you get into air press back and keep it pressed... because your backwheel would touch the obstacle".

### C2 Consecutive hops
- Lesson 10 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZxYWh6fTtxw: "use your bike's rotation and the force from landing to create the load on the rear suspension. sit back on the bike and land with your front wheel slightly earlier... when you hit that point shift your body weight forward and perform another bunny hop".

### C3 Rear wheel bounce
- Lesson 11 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohfMjoJvDV0: "only the rear wheel is powered, and therefore, only the rear wheel needs to be in contact" / "lean backwards and land on the rear wheel at roughly a 45° angle. If your front wheel dips down, lean backwards next time. And if you find yourself flipping backwards, lean forward a little." / "when the load is at its maximum, lean forward to jump".
- Trials 2 SE: "Leaning forward, with about 80 degree wheelie angle, (aka 'pogo stick' bounce)... transfers the downward motion to upward motion".
- Glossary https://trialscommunity.wordpress.com/techniques/: "Stationary Bunny Hop – Using a very intricate combination of leaning and throttle/brake to back wheel bounce in place."

### C4 Flat obstacles / slam / endo preload
- Lesson 9 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9f_hggzEvE: "slam the rear wheel and bike frame into the corner of the obstacle and then lean forward... your momentum will get you up and onto the obstacle" / "prior to performing a bunny hop Lean Forward... when you lean back there is extra pressure loaded on the rear suspension and... this increases the height" / "performing an endo before the jump will give you a huge boost".
- Kaiser Panda: [In the air lean forward to ~30–45°; when the front wheel touches, slam back, then quickly forward — front-wheel landing substitutes for missing run-up.]

### C5 Front wheel lift over lips
- Kaiser Panda "Slide": [pin the rider back to lift the front a few metres before the lip; once over it, lean hard forward to tip the bike over — avoids wasted air time.]
- Lesson 8 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U7DWi1Kd1IE: "accelerate at the base of the ramp but... ease off the gas only when your front wheel has cleared the obstacle lean forward... if you hold gas all the way to the top you'll be pushed backwards".

### C6 Landing
- Lesson 6 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HU5qgS4DhwE: "just as the bike meets the ramp lean forward to get your body weight over the handlebars... press the gas hard enough to stop you rolling backwards but gentle enough to avoid lifting your front wheel" / "the most common mistakes... accelerate too hard and flip backwards or... lean too far forward which will lift the rear wheel... if the rear wheel lifts you've got no power... tap the brake this will bring the rear wheel down".
- Lesson 3 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JMKEXDk_y00: "shifting your body weight will make the bike rotate in the direction of the lean gentle adjustments... don't forget to gently apply the gas to land in a better location".
- "land with your back wheel first and only slightly have the acceleration down... if you land both wheels together, you usually bounce off" https://steamcommunity.com/app/641080/discussions/0/1840188800793523991/
- "land on your back tire, at about a 20 degree angle to the ground... let you continue with speed even if you overshoot" — XboxAchievements Evo; "almost at a 90 degree angle with your back tire so that you don't slam down" (big drop).
- Trials 2 SE: "Leaning back on landing means stability. Most often used way." / "Back wheel landing... a decent spring assisted speed boost." / "Landing with a forward flip... a good speed boost from the back wheel kicking motion."
- Kaiser Panda: [both wheels together or rear slightly first; on steep slopes arrive rider-back, wheels parallel to the slope, then forward on contact; if the rear lifts, tap brake + lean back.]

### C7 Throttle control / wheelie / stability
- Lesson 1 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXp_OQadUIo: "full gas is [not] the fastest way... applying the gas gently... will also help you stay in control".
- Lesson 2 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-48hUANk3c: "going up a slope lean forward over the handlebars to stop the bike flipping... riding downhill... lean backwards".
- Lesson 8: "throttle control... use the gas very gently and only enough to maintain control without flipping backwards".
- Glossary: "Throttle Control – Precisely altering the amount of gas... climb steep inclines, and even vertical walls, without falling backwards." "Endo – Balancing on your front wheel." "Rug burn – Traveling forward on your back wheel while your back is touching or nearly touching the ground." "Squishy-Squashy – The act of leaning backward and forward quickly to keep momentum or to gain extra distance."
- Wheelie: "lean back with a little throttle to really get that front wheel up." / "Once in the wheelie, you can apply moderate gas to reach the required distance."
- Endo: "Apply a small amount of gas and then heavily brake to lift your back tire... apply a moderate rightward lean"; Oso https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U5gRNjJuG8U: "Lean Forward while holding down... brake... align yourself so that your back wheel and your head are roughly the same distance from the ground".
- Stability: "All the power in the bike comes from the back wheel... full gas... you'll often find yourself flipping over... applying some forward lean". "Leaning forward up hills is much more needed because your rear tire is what's moving you... Just lean into the hill right as you go up it and let off as you come off the top". Prima Fusion: "Lean forward... Don't forget to let go after coming up the hill, or else you'll end up in a nasty flip forward." https://primagames.com/tips/trials-fusion-beginners-tips
- Braking (Kaiser Panda): [lean back while braking; pump the brake in light rapid taps — a hard stab flips you forward.]
- "Don't go up a ramp at 100% speed, rather save the full gas until the final few feet of the ramp, as that will tilt the front wheel up".

### C8 Gas/brake in the air, "flapping", fender grab
- "The only way you can rotate the bike is with the brake and throttle, the brake will lean the bike forward whilst throttle will lean it backward." — PSNProfiles Fusion.
- "you can 'flap' by holding gas and tapping the brake, this will make it possible to frontflip/adjust your rotation forward." / "Hit brake off a jump to pitch the front end down".
- Lesson 8: "when only your front wheel is in contact with the object press brake this will allow your momentum to carry your bike over... rapidly tap brake while accelerating and your rear wheel should rise up... let's just call it flapping".
- Glossary: "Fapping – Rapidly tapping brake while holding gas to engage your engine which gives your back wheel more vertical momentum." / "Fendergrab – Catching yourself on a platform in-between your back wheel and fender, then holding gas to pull yourself up." / "Fronthook..." / "Boxing – Climbing a 90 degree angle wall from flat ground." / "Spiderman – Hanging on to the edge of a 90 degree object..."

### C9 Climbing / walls / transitions
- Ilvessuo: "with those steep inclines, need to go easy on the gas, easy on the leaning".
- Wall climbs: "the first landing on the wall" — rear wheel first, release throttle momentarily, accelerate once both wheels are on the wall https://steamcommunity.com/app/245490/discussions/0/492378806374379420
- Kaiser Panda "Transition" (= Lesson 5): approach rider-back; when the bike is mid-curve (front on the slope, rear on the flat, ~45°) snap forward to pin it to the slope; [if the rear lifts you were too slow or should have used light-forward not hard-forward].
- Oso "Slope to Vertical" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AQKWwBxWZ54: "your front wheel [will] exert too much force on the vertical wall causing an equal and opposite reaction... your suspension will kick in and force your front wheel back off the wall"; "accelerate sharply before my front wheel touches the wall this causes my front wheel to lift off the slope... when my back wheel is about to come into contact with the vertical I let off the throttle and gently make my way up the wall"; "we want to shift our weight in a direction that is as close to Vertical as we can manage".
- FatShady Inferno IV https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ysJ97Xdcr0A: "be centered over the bike before you attempt this... a seated position... change your body position it'll just give you a little bit more spring"; "if you're just sitting down here and accelerate that's 100 now nothing will happen... reverse just the touch and that'll give you the motion to go forward".
- Kaiser Panda: [a very light throttle press half a second before going full — the bike reacts differently].

### C10 Engine braking / coasting / flips
- Kaiser Panda: [on a steep descent, coasting exceeds full-throttle speed; accelerating downhill acts like braking = engine braking].
- GamesRadar Rising https://www.gamesradar.com/trials-rising-tips/: flips — "lean backwards or forwards just before you leave a ramp, then keep holding the lean... The longer you hold it, the faster your rotation speed will get... push in the opposite direction to slow your rotation". "easing off the gas or even dabbing the brakes to drop off the end of a big ramp, which will allow you to land quicker".
- Head crosses the line: [it is the rider's head that validates the finish and every checkpoint — lean forward on the line] — Kaiser Panda.

## D. Numbers found
- Evolution bars (IGN, /8): Rattler 2/2/2/2; Piranha 4/4/4/4; Scorpion 450 8/6/6/6; Phoenix Evo 250 6/8/8/8; Micro Donkey 6/6/6/8.
- Rising: LT 3 brake levels, RT 7 throttle levels.
- Angles: rear-wheel-bounce landing ~45° (Lesson 11); front-wheel landing lean 30–45°; transition snap ~45° bike angle; back-tyre landing ~20° for full-throttle no-flip; ~90° rear-tyre for a big drop; ~80° "pogo" bounce.
- "half of your throttle" on a landing; "tap the throttle (Around 65%)".
- Timing words: "very short"/"very briefly" uphill switch-jump taps, "longer" downhill; "a very light throttle press half a second before going full"; "lean softly for a second and let off" downhill; "left right and then potentially left again"; "a second or two longer to get up to speed" (450); "space to accelerate for more than two seconds".
- Fault penalty +5 s; 30 min / 500 fault limits (Rising).
