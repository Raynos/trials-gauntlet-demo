# Changed-groom correction 2 — final bounded conversion trial

The previous a2 source, recipe, GLB and report are preserved in ignored `attempts/a2/` with hashes. Face, eyes, skin and beard remain frozen A3. This correction has no acceptance claim.

## What was actually diagnosed

The old groom contained 25,000 buried centerline points out of 585,000 (4.27%). Crown points alone had 4,146 buried out of 208,926 (1.98%); the deepest penetration was 23.72mm. Penetration therefore existed but could not honestly be called the sole cause of the large crown gap.

The transfer now rotates each entire authored strand through source-scalp/target-head root normal frames. Only points below 1mm clearance are projected onto the actual evaluated head surface. All outside curls remain authored source geometry. The repeated evaluated-source check finds **zero buried centerline points**, including the crown. No opaque scalp shell or synthetic hair population was added.

A remaining conversion mismatch was investigated: a flat radial ribbon can disappear edge-on, while the author renders round curves. The same 9,000 strands now have thin three-sided cross-sections. This is deliberately expensive: **3,456,000 hair triangles**, a 91,811,840-byte GLB. It is an appearance experiment, not an accepted production or mobile representation.

Author Roll factor is reduced to 0.35 and Noise Distance to 0.035 source units; regional length fit limits side sweep and fringe loft. The dark-brown material uses more restrained highlights. Authored guide shape and clump identity remain derived from Bystedt's source.

The current transferred clump layout/coverage remains insufficient. More iterations on density, arbitrary normal interpretation or lighting would not establish the requested short tousled identity.
