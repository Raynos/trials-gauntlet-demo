Privacy Policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}} · applies to the Rockhop app on iPhone and Android and to this website

Rockhop collects no data about you. There are no accounts and no ads. There is no analytics and no tracking. The app needs no internet connection.

Who we are

Rockhop is an independent game made by Jake Verbaten ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, send, sell or share personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage data. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code, and no other company's code that does. It has no sign-in and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Rockhop saves your progress, best times, medals and settings (for example sound volume, graphics quality and your chosen bike) in the app's own storage on your phone. This is how the game remembers where you got to. That data never leaves your device through Rockhop, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you delete the app, or when you use Reset progress in the game's settings.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google's device backup, if you have it switched on) may include this app data as part of backing up your device. That happens under your Apple or Google account and their terms, not ours.

Network

Everything the game needs is inside the app. The app makes no network requests to us or to anyone else while you play.

This website

This site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard request information, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We do not add analytics, cookies or trackers to this site.

Children

Rockhop is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app collects none at all.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message and address only to reply. We delete them when the conversation is over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever started collecting data, we would update this policy and the store privacy labels before that version was released.
