Controls

Gas and brake
Right thumb, on the right-hand side of the touch strip at the bottom of the screen.
Lean
Left thumb, on the left-hand side. Leaning back lifts the front wheel; leaning forward keeps it down on climbs.
Hop
There is no hop button. Shift your weight back on the gas, then snap it forward, and the bike pops over the step.
Try again
Tap the restart button at the top right to go back to the last checkpoint at once. Hold it to restart the whole track.
Pause
Tap pause at the top left. On Android, the system back gesture or button also pauses a ride.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. The whole game is inside the app and plays offline.
Where is my progress saved?
On your phone, in the app's storage. Deleting the app deletes it. Settings → Reset progress clears it on purpose. There is no cloud save or account.
No sound?
On iPhone the game follows the ring/silent switch, so switch it to ring. Also check the volume slider in the game's settings.
The game feels slow on my phone
Open Settings and choose a lower graphics quality. The game also lowers quality by itself when a phone can't keep up. Closing other apps helps on older phones.
Is there anything to buy?
No. Rockhop is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
